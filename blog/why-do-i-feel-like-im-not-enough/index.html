Why Do I Feel Like I’m Not Enough?

By Jeff Batton · October 29, 2026

Short answer: the mirror is not lying to you. It is agreeing with you. You feel like you are not enough because that is the place you are looking from. This is how I say it in session: I’m not enough, so I keep looking at it from the place of I’m not enough, which just gets me more not enough, because that’s what the reflection in the mirror is, is that I’m not enough. The mirror is not cruel. The mirror is faithful. Change the place you stand, and the reflection has to change with it.

Running faster is still the same seed

Most people try to outrun the feeling. More effort, more proving, more showing up early and staying late. Here is the problem with that plan:

The more you need it, the further away it gets.

Jeff points to the old scriptural line about even the little you have being taken away, and his read on it is plain: the focus is on the lack, and so you just keep perpetuating the what’s missing.

Underneath the running is a small voice doing what it has always done. Look at me. Hey, I’m doing it as fast as I can. Look, I’m here. I’m doing it. See, I’m doing it. I’m showing up. That is not ambition. That is compensation. A person who believes they are enough does not have to announce it at full volume, all day, to everybody in the room.

And the running never arrives, because the seed is wrong. You cannot plant "I’m not enough" in the morning and harvest "enough" at night. Effort is not the issue. The seed is the issue.

You have to start planting corn

This is the turn, and it is simpler than the running ever was:

You have to start planting corn. I’m enough. That’s it.

What does enough actually sound like, said out loud? It sounds like this: I don’t need your validation. I don’t need your buy-in. I don’t need you to get this anymore. I don’t need you to see me anymore.

Read that again. It is not arrogance, and it is not giving up. It is a man or a woman standing in a different place. When you’re enough, you don’t need to show up to prove anything, because you’re not compensating for anything. The work still gets done. The love still gets given. It just stops being an audition.

Even your ordinary requests change. Said from not enough, a simple ask carries a whole story with it: nobody is thinking about me, I am being taken advantage of. Said from enough, the same ask is just practical.

Same words. Different seed. Different harvest.

How to start

First, catch the sentence. When "I’m not enough" starts running, do not argue with it and do not obey it. Just name the place you are looking from: this is the lack talking.

Second, quit feeding the lack for one day. No proving, no compensating, no running faster to be seen. Notice how loud the urge gets. That loudness is information, not instructions.

Third, plant the corn on purpose. Say it like you are putting seed in the ground, because you are: I’m enough. Then do the next thing in front of you from that place, and watch what the mirror hands back.

You will not feel it all at once. Seeds do not work that way. But a seed planted daily grows, and the old one, starved of your attention, does not.
