## Design Context

### Users
Adults seeking individual or couples coaching, often arriving after repeating relationship patterns. Visitors need to understand the offer, compare prices, book a conversation, and find helpful books between sessions.

### Brand Personality
Warm, candid, Southern. Saved JBLC brand notes describe an intimate, sophisticated study with a fireplace and mirror. Speak to one person; use concrete language and grounded humor.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the live site’s burgundy, warm charcoal, cream, and restrained gold. Keep its existing editorial typography for continuity. Add readable navigation and accessible layouts, without replacing the established visual identity.

### Design Principles
1. Make the offer and next step clear.
2. Keep promises proportionate to what coaching delivers.
3. Make pricing consistent with booking destinations.
4. Use real book covers and original summaries; disclose affiliate relationships.
5. Support keyboard use, readable contrast, reduced motion, and mobile layouts.
