What this is

What we offer is coaching — a conversational, exploratory service that helps you see your patterns more clearly and decide what to do about them. We offer individual and couples sessions online, hosted couples weekends with coaching in Jeff’s Detroit home, and speaking engagements. Weekend lodging is at Frederick Stearns House across the street, not in Jeff’s home.

If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, in danger, or in need of clinical care, please contact a licensed professional or, in an emergency, 911 or the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

Booking and payment

Individual sessions are $150 for 60 minutes and are scheduled through Calendly. A four-session package is $540 (10% off the standard session rate) and may be scheduled over time. Card payment is required to complete an online booking or package purchase, and a receipt is sent automatically to the email used at checkout. Existing clients may receive a private code that adjusts the single-session price at checkout. Couples sessions are $250 for 60 minutes and are arranged after an inquiry. A discovery conversation is free and can be requested through our inquiry form. Sending a discovery-call request does not book a paid session or authorize a charge.

Clients with recurring or pre-scheduled sessions may choose to keep a card on file through our secure payment processor. If you select this option and authorize it, Jeff Batton Life Coaching may charge that card on the day of each scheduled coaching session at your agreed session rate. Your card number is not stored on this website. You may withdraw this authorization for future charges by emailing jcbatton@gmail.com before the applicable cancellation deadline.

Hosted weekends are $4,500 per couple, including three nights’ lodging (Thursday through Saturday), coaching, meals, chef care, restaurant gratuities, and Sunday champagne brunch as described on the weekend page. Travel is the only excluded cost. Dates, room availability, the final schedule, and separate payment and cancellation terms are confirmed in writing before a reservation is accepted. Speaking fees and arrangements are agreed separately.

Cancellation and rescheduling

Life happens. Here’s the standard:

  • Before the deadline: cancel or reschedule individual and couples sessions by 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the day before the appointment without a late-cancellation charge.
  • After the deadline: the session fee applies. If the session has already been paid for, that payment is retained rather than charged again. For a prepaid package, one session credit is used.
  • No-shows: the same session fee applies.
  • Discovery calls: remain free. Please cancel if you cannot attend.
  • Weekend retreats: have separate cancellation and payment terms agreed before booking.

If something genuinely unforeseeable happens (medical emergency, family crisis), email jcbatton@gmail.com and we’ll do the human thing.
