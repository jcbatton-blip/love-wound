Here’s how a weekend
could unfold.

You don’t need to arrive knowing exactly what to say. We start with where you are. There’s time for honest conversation, time to catch your breath, and room to enjoy being together.

This is a sample Friday-to-Sunday format. We’ll agree on the schedule, location, and details together before you book.

01 · Friday

Arrive.
Settle in.

Put down the week. Make space for what brought you here.

  1. Late afternoon · opening conversation

    Where are we, really?

    We hear from each of you: what’s been happening, what feels hard to say, and what you hope this weekend makes room for. Together, we choose a starting point.

  2. Evening · time together

    Let the conversation breathe.

    A quiet dinner, a walk, or simply a little downtime together. Space to settle in without trying to solve everything on the first night.

02 · Saturday

Notice the pattern.
Try another way.

The heart of the weekend: understanding what keeps happening and practicing a different response.

  1. Morning · guided coaching

    What happens between you?

    We take a familiar moment and slow it down. What did you hear? What did you assume? What happened next? We look at the lessons about love that each of you brings into the room.

  2. Midday · a real break

    Rest. Eat. Take a walk.

    Time away from the conversation to reflect and let things settle. The whole day doesn’t need to feel like work.

  3. Afternoon · practice together

    Run the play differently.

    Bring one of those conversations you keep getting stuck in. With support, practice listening, asking for what you need, and noticing when the old response takes over.

  4. Evening · reconnect

    Remember there’s a person here.

    Unhurried time together, with a simple reflection to carry into the evening. There’s room for warmth, curiosity, and a little fun.

03 · Sunday

Bring it
back home.

Make the next step small enough to use in your actual life.

  1. Morning · reflect and put it into practice

    What are you seeing now?

    We revisit what stood out, what still feels difficult, and what you want to keep practicing. Each of you has room to say what you’re taking away.

  2. Before you leave · your next steps

    What will you do on Tuesday?

    Choose a few specific ways to respond when ordinary life gets busy again: a pause, a clearer request, a way to return to a difficult conversation. We can also discuss whether further coaching would be useful.

Something to
keep practicing.

The aim is to leave with a clearer understanding of each other and a few things you can actually use:

  • Language for the pattern you tend to fall into together.
  • Practice hearing each other without rushing to defend or fix.
  • A shared way to pause and return when a conversation gets difficult.
  • Specific next steps for the week ahead.

A weekend gives us time to begin. What grows from it takes practice.
