Here’s how a weekend
could unfold.

You don’t need to arrive knowing exactly what to say. We start with where you are. There’s time for honest conversation, time to catch your breath, and room to enjoy being together.

The preferred rhythm is Thursday evening through Sunday brunch. Friday and Saturday each have about two hours of morning conversation and a similar stretch in the afternoon—roughly eight hours across those two days, with plenty of breathing room. We’ll confirm the final schedule together.

01 · Thursday

Arrive.
Exhale.

An evening to settle in, without rushing straight into the work.

  1. Evening · arrival

    Your own space, across the street.

    Check in at Frederick Stearns House. Unpack, settle in, and let the travel day fall away. We’ll arrange dinner around your arrival; there’s no coaching agenda tonight.

02 · Friday

Coffee first.
Then conversation.

We begin in my home. There’s time to get comfortable and hear from each of you.

  1. 9 a.m. · coffee & conversation

    Where are we, really?

    Coffee, something to eat, and about two hours to talk. What’s been happening? What feels hard to say? What would you like this time together to make possible?

  2. Around noon · lunch & downtime

    Take your time.

    Lunch is taken care of. Then a walk, a rest, or some time together. You don’t need to spend every minute working on your relationship.

  3. Afternoon · conversation & practice

    Notice what keeps happening.

    We come back together for another unhurried stretch, around two hours. We slow a familiar conversation down and begin to see the pattern. Coffee and snacks are there when you want them.

  4. Evening · dinner in or out

    Let the conversation breathe.

    A meal prepared by the personal chef or a restaurant evening chosen for good food and unhurried conversation. We decide the details together, leaving room to enjoy yourselves.

03 · Saturday

See it.
Try another way.

The same easy rhythm, with room to go a little deeper.

  1. 9 a.m. · coffee & conversation

    What are you noticing?

    Another morning together, around two hours. We look at the expectations and old love scripts each of you brings into the room.

  2. Around noon · lunch & a real break

    Room to pause.

    Lunch at home or out, followed by generous downtime. Let the conversation settle. Rest, explore, or simply be together.

  3. Afternoon · practice together

    Run the play differently.

    Over another couple of hours, practice hearing each other, asking for what you need, and catching the familiar reaction before it takes over. The pace leaves room for pauses and refreshments.

  4. Evening · dinner & reconnect

    Remember there’s a person here.

    Good food, each other’s company, and a little fun. Dinner can be in with the personal chef or out at a restaurant. Everything is included.

04 · Sunday

Bring it
back home.

A few useful next steps, then a proper send-off.

  1. Morning · coffee & reflection

    What will you do on Tuesday?

    Coffee and a light breakfast, then time to reflect. Choose a few small ways to respond when ordinary life gets busy again: a pause, a clearer request, a way to return to a difficult conversation.

  2. Before departure · champagne brunch

    End on a good note.

    We go out for a leisurely Sunday champagne brunch before you leave. Good food and time to enjoy being together. We’ll coordinate departure plans in advance so the last morning stays unhurried.

Something to
keep practicing.

The aim is to leave with a clearer understanding of each other and a few things you can actually use:

  • Language for the pattern you tend to fall into together.
  • Practice hearing each other without rushing to defend or fix.
  • A shared way to pause and return when a conversation gets difficult.
  • Specific next steps for the week ahead.

A weekend gives us time to begin. What grows from it takes practice.

The practical details

Let’s talk it through first.

The investment

The weekend rate is $4,500 per couple. That covers Thursday, Friday, and Saturday nights at Frederick Stearns House, coaching in my home, personal chef care, coffee, snacks, meals in or out, restaurant gratuities, and Sunday champagne brunch. The only additional cost is your travel.

After your inquiry, we’ll talk about fit and dates. You’ll receive the proposed room, schedule, all-inclusive arrangements, and payment and cancellation terms in writing before you decide. Sending an inquiry does not reserve dates or commit you to payment.

Is this right for us?

Start with a conversation about what you’re hoping for and whether this format fits. You can ask questions and get a feel for working with me before making a decision.

This is coaching: a place to look at your patterns and practice how you want to respond. It isn’t a promise to resolve everything in one weekend.
