Books for the work

The syllabus.

A little reading. A different way of seeing.
Books on awareness, love, and the life you’re ready to live.

No required reading. No need to buy them all. Start with the one that speaks to you.

Seeing yourself clearly

Awareness

Anthony de Mello

Drawing on stories and spiritual traditions, de Mello challenges the beliefs and habits we mistake for who we are. His invitation is to notice our reactions, question our assumptions, and become more awake to everyday life.

View on Amazon ↗

Freedom and trust

Summerhill School

A. S. Neill

Neill describes an approach to education centered on children’s freedom, emotional well-being, and shared responsibility. Beyond the classroom, the book invites questions about authority, approval, and how people learn to trust themselves.

View on Amazon ↗

Love and relationships

A Return to Love

Marianne Williamson

Williamson reflects on A Course in Miracles and the choice between fear and love. She brings that spiritual perspective to relationships, forgiveness, work, and the ways we search for peace in ordinary life.

View on Amazon ↗

Letting go of control

The Surrender Experiment

Michael A. Singer

In this memoir, Singer follows an experiment in loosening his grip on how life should unfold. From solitude to building a business, he explores what happens when personal preferences stop being the only guide to his decisions.

View on Amazon ↗

Beginning the work

The War of Art

Steven Pressfield

Pressfield calls the force behind procrastination and self-doubt “Resistance.” In short, direct chapters, he explores how to recognize it and build the discipline to keep showing up for creative work and meaningful change.

View on Amazon ↗

Understanding old patterns

The Body Keeps the Score

Bessel van der Kolk

A psychiatrist’s account of how trauma can affect memory, relationships, and the body. Drawing on research and clinical stories, van der Kolk discusses approaches to recovery. This is a substantial, sometimes difficult read about traumatic experiences.

View on Amazon ↗

A little of my story

Grinnin’ Like a Jackass Eatin’ Briars

Jeff Batton with Landon J. Napoleon

Stories from a Southern childhood and a life of travel, told with humor and candor. Jeff’s memoir explores the experiences that shaped his perspective on love, awareness, and learning to be at home in your own life.
