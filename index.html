What happens

Sixty minutes. Three movements.

We move slowly and we move directly. By the end of the call you won't have a plan — you'll have a seeing. That's the thing worth booking for.

i. Min. 0–20

We identify your Love Wound.

You tell me the pattern — in love, in work, or in both. I listen for the shape underneath the story. We name it, out loud, in language you can carry home. Most people have never heard it said back to them.

ii. Min. 20–50

We trace it to its origin.

We go back — not to perform the past, but to find the first seven years where the blueprint was written. This is the part therapy often circles and rarely lands on. Here, we land on it.

iii. Min. 50–60

You leave with clarity you can act on.

Not a worksheet. Not homework. One clear thing you now see that you didn't see this morning — and the shape of the next step, whether or not you ever work with me again.

That same day —

You'll carry a short written reflection from the session. One page. The thing we named, in my hand, so it can't be unsaid.

You can't kill a tree by pulling the fruit off of it. The work isn't fixing your behavior — it's finding the root.

— Jeff
