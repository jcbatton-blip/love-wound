Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

This is the privacy policy for Jeff Batton Life Coaching LLC ("we," "us," or "Jeff"), a one-person coaching practice based in Detroit, Michigan. It's written in plain English because the law shouldn't be the only thing in the room that's hard to understand.

What we collect

When you interact with this site or book a session, we may receive:

  • Your name and email address (when you book a call or session)
  • Scheduling details — date, time, time zone (via Calendly)
  • Payment information — handled and stored by Stripe; we never see or store your card number
  • Anything you choose to share with us in a session, by email, or in a form
  • Basic site analytics — pages visited, anonymized device info — used to improve the site, not to identify you

How we use it

We use the information you give us only for the obvious things:

  • To deliver the coaching service you booked
  • To send you confirmations, reminders, and the written reflection that follows a Mirror Session
  • To answer your questions and stay in touch when you ask us to
  • To keep our practice running — accounting, scheduling, basic compliance

We do not sell your data. We do not rent it. We do not pass it to advertisers.

Third parties we use

This site relies on a handful of trusted services to operate:

  • Calendly — handles scheduling and calendar coordination
  • Stripe — processes payments; your card data lives with them, not us
  • Netlify — hosts this website
  • Email — Gmail / Google Workspace for ongoing correspondence

Each of these has their own privacy policy, and we recommend reading them if you want the full picture.

Confidentiality of session content

What you share in a coaching session stays between you and Jeff. We do not record sessions, we do not share session notes with third parties, and we don't discuss specific client material publicly.

Two narrow exceptions, common to any helping professional: (1) if you indicate you intend to seriously harm yourself or someone else, and (2) if we are legally compelled by a court order. Beyond that, what is said in the room stays in the room.

Your rights

You can ask us, at any time, to:

  • Tell you what information we have about you
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete your data from our records (except where we're required to keep it for tax or legal reasons)
  • Stop emailing you

Email jcbatton@gmail.com with the request and we'll handle it within a reasonable window — usually a few business days.

Cookies

This site uses minimal cookies — only what's needed for the site to function and to understand basic anonymized traffic. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising.

Children

This service is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be flagged plainly.

Contact

Questions, requests, or anything else: jcbatton@gmail.com.
